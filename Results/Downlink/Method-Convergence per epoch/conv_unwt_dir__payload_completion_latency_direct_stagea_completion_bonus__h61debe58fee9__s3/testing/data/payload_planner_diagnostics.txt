Downlink payload planner diagnostics

Terms
Active users: users with payload remaining at the start of that block.
Mask: the subset of active users jointly optimized for service in that block.
Immediate latency: current-block latency contribution after any successful tail n_kl reduction.
Value-to-go: continuation surrogate built from remaining bits divided by each user's historical solo-preview service level.
Opportunity penalty: penalty for skipping or underusing a user whose current solo block looks unusually good for that user.
